    Every home starts flat. Ours didn't stay that way.

    We started from the builder's E2-02 plan and changed what mattered most to us: the kitchen. The store became part of a U-shaped kitchen, the sink became a Futura multi-function sink, and the purifier moved under the counter. There's also a point for the robot that mops the floor.

    1. 01Kitchen redesigned as per option 1.
    2. 02Purifier inlet and electrical point moved for an under-counter purifier.
    3. 03Futura multi-function sink instead of the standard sink.
    4. 04PP3 power point moved to 600 mm from floor (switch on SB13).
    5. 05Extra 16 A point for the mopping robot dock, 1 ft from floor.
